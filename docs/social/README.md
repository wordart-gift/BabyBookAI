# Social profiles: what to upload

Kit for Instagram (`instagram.com/baby.bookai`) and YouTube
(`youtube.com/@BabyBookAI`), in English (the US is the main market). The
images in this folder are generated from the app icon and the site's own
material; regenerate them rather than editing by hand.

Every claim here is true of the product today: Android now, iPhone coming,
the app is free and the printed book is paid, the photos are chosen on the
phone. No ratings, no "layflat", no face recognition as a selling point
(see the site's CLAUDE.md).

## Instagram

- **Profile photo:** `profile-instagram-1080.png` (Instagram crops it to a
  circle; the stork and the book stay inside).
- **Name** (30 characters max, searchable): `BabyBookAI · Baby photo book`
- **Bio** (150 max):

  ```
  Your baby's first year, made into a printed photo book by our own AI 📖
  Free app · Android now, iPhone soon
  👇 Make yours
  ```

- **Link:** `https://babybookai.com/` (or, to track Instagram visits,
  `https://babybookai.com/get/?src=instagram`, which sends phones straight to
  the store).
- **Category** (professional account): Product/service. Contact email:
  `info@babybookai.com`.

### The first three posts (4:5, 1080 × 1350)

Post them in this order; the grid shows the newest first.

1. `instagram-post-1-the-book.png`

   ```
   You took hundreds of photos this year. We'll make the book.

   BabyBookAI goes through your camera roll on your phone, picks the best
   photos of every month and lays out a hardcover photo book of your baby's
   first year. You flip through, swap anything you don't love, and it's
   printed and shipped to your door.

   Free on Google Play. iPhone coming soon. Link in bio.

   #babyphotobook #firstyear #babysfirstyear #photobook #babyalbum
   #newmom #newparents #babymemories #firstbirthday #momlife
   ```

2. `instagram-post-2-how-it-works.png`

   ```
   Three steps, and you only do the easy one.

   1. Tell it your baby's name and the dates.
   2. Our own AI picks the photos from your camera roll, month by month.
      Nothing is uploaded to choose them.
   3. Flip through, swap a photo for another from the same month, order.

   No weekly prompts, no journaling, no subscription.

   #babybook #babyphotoalbum #photobook #newmom #parentinghacks
   #babysfirstyear #firsttimemom
   ```

3. `instagram-post-3-first-and-last-page.png`
